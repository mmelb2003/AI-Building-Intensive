# Console

Dark, precise, a little technical. Near-black screen, one cyan accent, mono for labels. Feels like a product for people who work with data.

**Use this when:** developer tools, analytics, logs, ops, anything that should feel sharp and focused.

## Colors
- Background: `#0B1220`
- Surface / cards: `#151C2C`
- Text: `#E6EDF7`
- Muted text: `#8B9BB4`
- Border: `#2A3550`
- Brand: `#22D3EE`
- Brand hover: `#67E8F9`
- Brand on color: `#0B1220`
- Success: `#34D399`
- Danger: `#FB7185`

## Type
- Body / UI: Inter or system-ui, sans-serif
- Labels, code, IDs, timestamps: JetBrains Mono or ui-monospace
- Page title: 24–28px, weight 600
- Section title: 18px, weight 600
- Body: 15px, line-height 1.5
- Mono labels: 12–13px

## Shape and space
- Radius: 8px
- No glow, no neon gradients
- Hairline borders, not heavy shadows
- Page padding: 20–24px
- Dense but not cramped: 16px between related items, 28px between sections
- Max content width: 1120px

## Components
- **Nav:** dark sidebar or compact top bar, cyan only for the current item
- **Primary button:** cyan fill, dark text
- **Secondary button:** transparent, cyan or muted border
- **Cards:** darker surface, 1px border, no drop shadow
- **Tables / lists:** compact rows, mono for IDs and numbers
- **Status:** small pills — green success, pink danger, cyan info

## Do
- High contrast text on dark
- Use cyan as a signal, not a wash
- Align numbers and keep tables tidy

## Don’t
- Pure black `#000` full-page (too harsh)
- Rainbow charts or purple-pink gradients
- Light mode
- Playful illustration
